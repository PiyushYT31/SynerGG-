# **App Name**: BGMI Synergy Connect

## Core Features:

- Org Profile & Roster Management: Organizations can create verified profiles with logos, social links, and achievements, manage their 5-man roster, and update slot availability (e.g., vacant, open for replacement).
- Player Pro-Profile Builder: Players can create detailed profiles including core stats (F/D ratio), role, experience, provide POV reel links (YouTube/Drive), and define 'ideal teammate' criteria using free text for synergy matching.
- Intelligent Synergy Matching Tool: Calculates a dynamic 'Match Score' between player and organization profiles based on expressed preferences (player's 'Ideal Teammates', org's 'Team Style') using a generative AI tool to understand and quantify compatibility.
- Recruitment Dashboard (Orgs): Organizations can browse and filter potential players by role, playstyle, device, and synergy score. They can then send one-click 'Tryout Requests' to promising candidates.
- Org Discovery & Application (Players): Players can browse a marketplace of organizations with open slots, view detailed org profiles and their synergy scores, and apply directly to teams that match their criteria.
- Secure User Authentication: Enables secure signup and login for both organization managers and individual players.
- Tryout Request & Communication: A system for managing tryout requests, allowing both players and organizations to send, receive, accept, and decline invitations, facilitating initial communication.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background color: Deep, desaturated charcoal (#141D1F), establishing a 'Gamer Dark Mode' aesthetic. This subtly blue-green tinted dark background ties visually to the primary while remaining minimalist.
- Primary accent color: A vibrant, electric cyan (#1FE1F7), used for key interactive elements, headlines, and critical information to create a dynamic and tech-savvy feel.
- Secondary accent color: A tactical green-cyan (#4FC79D), analogous to the primary but with distinct saturation and brightness, providing contrast for secondary actions or highlighted data.
- Headlines: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern, techy, and slightly futuristic aesthetic, fitting the 'hacker' vibe.
- Body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for clean readability of stats, descriptions, and longer form content, complementing the headline font without being distracting.
- Distinct and unique icons for each player role (e.g., a shield for Support, a crosshair for Entry Fragger, an eye for IGL) to enhance quick visual recognition.
- Integrated video previews: Display small thumbnail previews for POV reel links on player cards, allowing organizations to instantly assess gameplay without navigating away.
- Subtle interactive animations on hover and click states for buttons, cards, and list items, enhancing the 'gamer mode' feel without overwhelming the user.
